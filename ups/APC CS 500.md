# APC Back-UPS CS 500

## SPECS

### General & Electrical Specs

* **Model Numbers:** BK500 / BK500BLK (120V US) | BK500EI (230V Intl)
* **Topology:** Standby (Off-Line)
* **Output Capacity:** 500 VA / 300 Watts
* **Output Waveform (on Battery):** Stepped Approximated Sine Wave
* **Input Voltage Range:** 82V – 144V AC (120V Nominal)
* **Transfer Time:** 6 ms (typical), 10 ms (max)
* **Surge Protection Rating:** 480 Joules

### Outlets & Interfaces

* **Battery + Surge Outlets:** 3x NEMA 5-15R
* **Surge-Only Outlets:** 3x NEMA 5-15R
* **Data Line Protection:** RJ-45 / RJ-11 (Phone / Fax / DSL)
* **Management Port:** USB / RS-232 (via RJ-45 to USB cable) for PowerChute / NUT monitoring

### Battery & Runtime

* **Battery Type:** Sealed Lead-Acid (SLA), 12V 7.5Ah / 9Ah
* **Replacement Battery Cartridge:** APC RBC2
* **Typical Recharge Time:** 8 hours
* **Half-Load Runtime (150W):** ~11.4 minutes
* **Full-Load Runtime (300W):** ~2.4 minutes

### Physical Specs

* **Dimensions (H × W × D):** 6.50 in × 3.58 in × 11.18 in (165 mm × 91 mm × 284 mm)
* **Weight:** 13.8 lbs (6.27 kg)
* **Form Factor:** Mini-Tower
